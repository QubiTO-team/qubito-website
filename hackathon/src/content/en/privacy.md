*Last updated: [date]*

This notice explains how we handle your personal data when you apply to the Qubito Hackathon (11–13 December 2026, Torino) through our application form, or write to us as a potential partner, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation 2016/679).

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

QubiTO, the quantum computing student team of Politecnico di Torino, [address].
Email: teamqubito@gmail.com

## 2. What data we collect

**If you apply to the hackathon** (through our Google Form):
- name, surname and email address;
- current level of study or work, field of study or work, university or organisation;
- your self-assessment, from 1 to 5, in a few areas (quantum mechanics, quantum information, quantum software, algorithms, hardware, software development);
- your motivation statement and any extra information you choose to add;
- dietary restrictions or allergies, only if you choose to tell us;
- your choices on the consent boxes (privacy notice, and optionally photos and videos);
- the date and time of your application.

**If you write to us as a potential partner:** the information in your email (usually your name, company and contact details).

We do not ask for documents, CVs, phone numbers or ID numbers.

## 3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Reviewing applications, selecting participants and telling you the result | Your request to take part (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) |
| Sending you the automatic confirmation email and practical information about the event | Your request to take part (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Preparing meals that are safe for you | Your explicit consent, given by filling in the optional dietary field (Art. 9(2)(a)) |
| Publishing photos or videos in which you appear on our website and social media | Your consent, given with the optional photo box (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| Answering partner requests | Our legitimate interest in organising the event with partners (Art. 6(1)(f)) |

Giving us the data marked as required is necessary to apply. Without it we cannot consider your application. Dietary information and photo consent are optional and do not affect selection.

We do not make automated decisions about you: applications are read and evaluated by people.

## 4. Who can see your data

- The members of QubiTO who organise the hackathon.
- Google, which provides the tools we use to collect and store applications and to send emails (Google Forms, Google Sheets and Gmail). Google acts on our behalf and may process data outside the EU under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
- The catering service, only for the dietary needs required to prepare meals, without your other application data.
- Politecnico di Torino, only the names of participants if required for access to the building during the event.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with sponsors or partners. Partners will not receive your contact details unless you give them yourself.

## 5. Photos and videos

During the event we may take photos and videos. We publish images in which you can be recognised only if you ticked the photo box. You can withdraw that consent at any time by writing to us, and we will remove the images from our channels where possible. If you prefer not to appear, tell the organisers on site.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Applications: until [31 March 2027], then deleted.
- Dietary information: deleted within one week after the event.
- Partner requests: up to [2 years], so we can contact the company for future editions, unless you ask us to delete them earlier.
- Published photos and videos: as long as they remain on our channels, unless you withdraw consent.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect what we did before. Write to teamqubito@gmail.com and we will reply within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

## 8. This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. Our hosting provider (Politecnico di Torino) may keep technical server logs, such as IP addresses, for security purposes.
