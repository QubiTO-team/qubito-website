The QubiTO Hackathon is the quantum computing hackathon organised by our team: three days at Politecnico di Torino that bring together students, researchers and companies.

On 11 December the partner companies present their work and the real problems behind the challenges. On 12 December teams of four are formed and 24 hours of hacking begin, to solve the challenges with quantum algorithms and simulators. On 13 December the solutions are presented and the winners are awarded.

Anyone can apply: bachelor's, master's and PhD students, researchers and professionals, from any university or organisation. You don't need to know quantum computing already, participation is free and the event is in English. Programme, practical info and applications are on the hackathon website.
