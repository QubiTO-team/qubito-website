Our first major outreach event at Politecnico di Torino, organised in collaboration with BeQuantum: a great success, with over 200 attendees in the room.

After Prof. Bartolomeo Montrucchio presented the quantum study paths at the Politecnico, a round table with Luca Crippa (IBM), Davide Corbelletto (Intesa Sanpaolo) and Noemi Ferrari (AT Agency) discussed the new professional roles in quantum technologies. Then the floor passed to the audience.
