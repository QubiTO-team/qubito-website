A quantum hackathon

QubiTO, the quantum computing student team of Politecnico di Torino, brings together students, researchers and companies for three days. First, partner companies present real problems. Then teams have 24 hours to solve them with quantum algorithms and simulators.

FAQ

Who can apply?

Everyone: bachelor's, master's and PhD students, researchers and professionals, from any university or organisation. You don't need to be at Politecnico di Torino.

Do I need to know quantum computing?

No. Curiosity is enough: we look for mixed teams, and mentors will help you get started.

Does it cost anything?

Participation is free, and all meals are included: dinner on 11 December, breakfast, lunch and dinner on 12 December, breakfast and lunch on 13 December.

Do I need a team to apply?

No. Everyone applies individually, and teams of 4 are formed on site at the start of the event.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, an ID and whatever you need to survive 24 hours.

What language is the event in?

English. Talks, challenges and presentations are all in English.
