Where

  • Company talks, 11 December: Politecnico di Torino, Corso Duca degli Abruzzi 24, 10129 Torino (map).
  • Hackathon, 12–13 December: Politecnico di Torino, Corso Duca degli Abruzzi 24 (map).
  • Dinner, 11 December: Comala, Corso Francesco Ferrucci 63, 10138 Torino (map). Open to everyone, participants included.

Getting there

  • By train: from Torino Porta Nuova the Politecnico is about 15 minutes on foot; from Torino Porta Susa about 25 minutes on foot.
  • By public transport: plan your trip with the GTT journey planner or Google Maps, using the map links above.

Check-in

Check-in is on 12 December between 8:30 and 9:00. Bring a valid ID and your confirmation email. Teams of 4 are formed right after check-in, before the challenges are presented at 10:00.

What to bring

  • Laptop and charger (a power strip is a good idea too).
  • ID document.
  • Water bottle, a sweater for the night, toiletries.

Food is on us: dinner on 11 December, breakfast, lunch and dinner on 12 December, breakfast and lunch on 13 December. If you told us about dietary restrictions, we’ll take care of them.

During the night

Organisers are on site for the whole night. We’ll give you the details about the night (where to rest, leaving and re-entering the building) on site, or by email to accepted participants before the event.

Contacts

  • Before and after the event: teamqubito@gmail.com
  • During the event: talk to any organiser. We’ll share a contact number on site.
